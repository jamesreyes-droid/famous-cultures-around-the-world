Famous Brazil: Carnival & Football 巴西狂歡與足球

Part 1 📍 The Country 國家

Brazil is a big and warm country in South America南美洲 🌎. It is famous for the Amazon rainforest, sunny beaches, and happy people!

🇨🇳 巴西是位於南美洲的一個又大又溫暖的國家。它以亞馬遜熱帶雨林、陽光明媚的海灘和熱情快樂的人們而聞名！
Part 2 🎭 Grand Festival 狂歡節

Every year, millions of people celebrate 🎭 Carnival. Dancers wear colorful costumes服裝 👗 with feathers and dance Samba to lively drums. It is the biggest street party in the world!

🇨🇳 每年有數百萬人慶祝 Carnival (嘉年華狂歡節)。舞者穿著帶有羽毛的色彩鮮艷服裝，隨著輕快的鼓聲跳桑巴舞。這是全球最大的街頭派對！
Part 3 ⚽ National Sport 足球運動

Brazil is also crazy about ⚽ Football! Kids and adults play on the beach, in streets, and in giant stadiums體育場 🏟️. Brazil has won the World Cup five times!

🇨🇳 巴西也對 Football (足球) 極度狂熱！小孩和大人在海灘上、街道上和巨大的體育場裡踢球。巴西曾獲得五次世界盃冠軍！
Part 4 ✨ Fun Summary 快樂結語

🎭 Carnival and ⚽ Football fill Brazil with music, joy, and energy. Welcome to join the celebration in Brazil!

🇨🇳 Carnival 與 Football 讓巴西充滿了音樂、歡樂與活力。歡迎來到巴西加入這場盛大的慶典！
