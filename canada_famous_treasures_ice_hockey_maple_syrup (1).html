Welcome to Canada! (加拿大大觀)

Canada is a very big country in North America.

加拿大是北美洲一個非常大的國家。

In winter, it is very cold and white with snow.

在冬天，這裡非常寒冷，到處是一片白雪。

Canadian people love outdoor sports and sweet food!

加拿大人民非常喜愛戶外運動與美味的甜食！

Ice Hockey & The Rink (冰上曲棍球與滑冰場)

Ice Hockey is the most popular sport in Canada.

冰上曲棍球是加拿大最受歡迎的運動。

Players put on ice skates and slide on the frozen rink very fast.

球員穿上滑冰鞋，在冰凍的溜冰場上快速滑行。

They use sticks to hit a black puck into the net to score points.

他們用球桿將黑色的冰球擊入網中來得分。

Maple Syrup & Sweet Pancakes (楓糖漿與美味鬆餅)

Canada is also famous for delicious Maple Syrup.

加拿大也以美味的楓糖漿而聞名遐邇。

People collect sap from maple trees in spring and boil it.

人們在春天從楓樹採集樹汁並將其煮沸濃縮。

The syrup is very sweet and yummy on hot pancakes!

這種糖漿非常甜美，淋在熱氣騰騰的鬆餅上超好吃！
