Short Story 簡短文章

Nigeria's Treasures 尼日利亞的寶藏

Part 1 📍 Country 國家

Nigeria is a colorful country in West Africa西非 🌍. People love food and music!

🇨🇳 尼日利亞是西非一個色彩繽紛的國家。那裡的人們熱愛美食與音樂！
Part 2 🍲 Famous Food 著名美食

One famous food is 🍚 Jollof Rice. It is red, spicy香辣的 🌶️, and yummy. People eat it at big parties!

🇨🇳 一道著名的食物是 Jollof Rice (朱洛夫飯)。它是紅色的、香辣又好吃。人們會在大型派對上吃它！
Part 3 🎵 Famous Music 著名音樂

Another famous thing is 🎵 Afrobeats music. It has fast drums and happy rhythms節奏 🥁. It makes everyone want to dance跳舞 💃!

🇨🇳 另一個聞名的事物是 Afrobeats (非洲拍子音樂)。它有快速的鼓聲和歡快的節奏。它讓每個人都想跳舞！
Part 4 ✨ Fun Summary 快樂結語

🍚 Jollof Rice and 🎵 Afrobeats bring joy! Nigeria is full of fun!

🇨🇳 Jollof Rice 和 Afrobeats 帶來歡樂！尼日利亞充滿樂趣！
