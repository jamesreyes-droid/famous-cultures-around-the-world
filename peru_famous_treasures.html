Discover Peru: Alpacas & Machu Picchu 探索秘魯

Part 1 📍 Welcome to Peru 歡迎來到秘魯

Peru is a beautiful country in South America南美洲 🌎. It has high mountains called the Andes, deep green forests, and rich history!

🇨🇳 秘魯是位於南美洲的一個美麗國家。它擁有被稱為安地斯山脈的高山、深綠色的森林以及豐富的歷史！
Part 2 🦙 Alpaca and Llama 羊駝與駱馬

In the mountains, you can see fluffy 🦙 Alpacas & Llamas. Alpacas give soft wool羊毛/毛料 🧶 to make warm clothes, and llamas help carry bags. They are super friendly and famous animals of Peru!

🇨🇳 在山區裡，你可以看到毛茸茸的 Alpaca (羊駝) 與 Llama (駱馬)。羊駝提供柔軟的毛來製作保暖的衣服，而駱馬則能幫忙馱負背包。牠們是秘魯非常友善且著名的動物！
Part 3 🏔️ Machu Picchu 馬丘比丘

High up in the clouds sits the ancient city of 🏔️ Machu Picchu. It was built with giant stone blocks by the Inca Empire印加帝國 👑 long ago. People call it the Lost City because it was hidden in the mountains for hundreds of years!

🇨🇳 高聳在雲端之上的是古老的 Machu Picchu (馬丘比丘) 城。它是很久以前由印加帝國用巨大的石塊建造而成的。人們稱它為「失落之城」，因為它隱藏在群山之中長達數百年！
Part 4 ✨ Great Wonder 偉大的奇蹟

🦙 Alpacas and 🏔️ Machu Picchu make Peru a magical place to explore. Welcome to visit the amazing wonders of Peru!

🇨🇳 Alpaca 與 Machu Picchu 讓秘魯成為一個神奇的探索勝地。歡迎前來體驗秘魯讓人驚嘆的奇蹟！
