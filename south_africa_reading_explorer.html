Paragraph 1: Welcome to South Africa!

🌍 Rainbow Nation

South Africa南非 is a big and sunny country at the southern tip of Africa. ☀️

南非是位於非洲最南端的大型且充滿陽光的國家。

🏔️ Nature & Oceans

It has high mountains, long beaches, and two oceans! 🌊

它擁有高山、長沙灘以及兩座大洋！

🦁 Famous Animals

It is famous for amazing nature and wild animals. 🐘

它以壯麗的大自然和野生動物聞名。

🛻

Paragraph 2: Going on a Safari Famous Icon #1

🛻 Wildlife Tour

Many visitors go on a Safari ⭐野生探險獵遊 in big national parks. 🌳

許多遊客會在大型國家公園裡體驗野生探險獵遊（Safari）。

🦁 Big Animals

You can ride a Jeep to see lions獅子, elephants大象, and rhinos犀牛. 🦏

你可以搭乘吉普車觀看獅子、大象和犀牛。

📸 Safari Fun

Seeing wild animals up close on a Safari ⭐野生探險獵遊 is exciting and fun! 📷

在 Safari 探險中近距離觀察野生動物非常刺激有趣！

🏉

Paragraph 3: Rugby - South Africa's Favorite Sport Famous Icon #2

🏆 National Sport

Rugby ⭐英式橄欖球 is the most popular sport in South Africa. 🏉

英式橄欖球是南非最受歡迎的運動。

🦌 Springboks Team

The national team隊伍 is called the Springboks跳羚隊 (南非橄欖球國家隊). 🦌

國家隊被稱為「跳羚隊」（Springboks）。

🏟️ Rugby Stadium

Players wear green jerseys and play Rugby ⭐英式橄欖球 with an oval ball橢圓形球 in a big stadium體育場. 🎉

球員身穿綠色球衣，在大體育場裡用橢圓形球進行橄欖球比賽。
