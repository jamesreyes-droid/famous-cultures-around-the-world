Famous Spain: Bullfighting & Flamenco 西班牙風采

Part 1 📍 The Country 國家

Spain is a beautiful country in southwestern Europe歐洲 🌍. It is famous for sunny weather, rich history, and exciting culture!

🇨🇳 西班牙是位於歐洲西南部的一個美麗國家。它以陽光明媚的天氣、豐富的歷史和刺激的文化而聞名！
Part 2 🐂 Traditional Sport 傳統鬥牛

One famous tradition is 🐂 Bullfighting. In the ring, a brave matador鬥牛士 🚩 wears a colorful cape and shows great courage with a strong bull. It is an old Spanish tradition that many people know!

🇨🇳 一個著名的傳統是 Bullfighting (斗牛)。在競技場中，勇敢的鬥牛士揮舞著鮮豔的斗篷，與強壯的公牛展現無畏的勇氣。這是大家都知道的古老西班牙傳統！
Part 3 💃 Passionate Dance 熱情舞蹈

Another famous art form is 💃 Flamenco. Dancers wear red dresses, clap their hands, and stamp their feet to fast guitar吉他 🎸 music. It is full of energy and passion!

🇨🇳 另一個著名的藝術形式是 Flamenco (弗明哥舞)。舞者身穿紅裙，隨著快速的吉他音樂拍手並踏腳。這充滿了能量與熱情！
Part 4 ✨ Fun Summary 快樂結語

🐂 Bullfighting and 💃 Flamenco make Spain famous across the world. Welcome to feel the rhythm and spirit of Spain!

🇨🇳 Bullfighting 與 Flamenco 讓西班牙聞名全球。歡迎來感受西班牙的節奏與靈魂！
