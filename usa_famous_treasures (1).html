Famous USA: Movies & Basketball 美國風采

Part 1 📍 The Country 國家

The USA is a big country in North America北美洲 🌎. It is famous all around the world!

🇨🇳 美國是北美洲的一個大國。它在全世界都非常聞名！
Part 2 🎬 Movies 電影

One famous place is ⭐ Hollywood. It makes exciting movies電影 🍿 with superhero stars明星 🌟. People love watching Hollywood films!

🇨🇳 一個著名的景點是 Hollywood (好萊塢)。它製作帶有超級英雄明星的刺激電影。人們很喜歡看好萊塢電影！
Part 3 🏀 Basketball 籃球

Another famous thing is the 🏀 NBA. It is the top basketball籃球 ⛹️ league with amazing players. Fans cheer loudly for their teams!

🇨🇳 另一個聞名的事物是 NBA (美國職籃)。它是擁有頂尖球員的最高殿堂籃球聯賽。球迷們熱情地為隊伍加油！
Part 4 ✨ Fun Summary 快樂結語

⭐ Hollywood and the 🏀 NBA make the USA special. They bring fun and magic to everyone!

🇨🇳 Hollywood 與 NBA 讓美國特別與眾不同。它們帶給每個人樂趣與魔法！
